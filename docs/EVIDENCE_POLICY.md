# Evidence policy — shared proposal

Canonical evidence and research registers remain in their owning repository. Coordination should link to them, not create a second source of truth.

Each material claim should include source, exact revision, scope, method, observations, limitations, reproducibility conditions and potential disconfirming evidence.

Suggested review labels: **RECORDED**, **REVIEW_PENDING**, **CONFIRMED**, **CONFIRMED_WITH_LIMITATIONS**, **NOT_REPRODUCED**, **SUPERSEDED**. These are coordination labels, not automatic scientific approvals.

When reviews disagree, record the discrepancy and reconcile before declaring closure. A dashboard or checkpoint cannot override corrected canonical evidence. Green technical tests do not establish scientific capabilities.
